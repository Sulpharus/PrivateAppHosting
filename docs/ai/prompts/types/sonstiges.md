## App type: other

Derive the views from the user's description. Keep to the app shell of the design system:
two to five views in the tab bar, the most used view first, and one primary add action. Use
tiles for things with pictures, lists for things people compare, and a detail sheet plus an
editor sheet for each kind of item.
